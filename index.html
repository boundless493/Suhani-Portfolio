01 // About Me

A little introduction to the artist behind the work.

Dedicated to mastering the dialogue between traditional art, visual design, and emotional communication.

Hello! I'm Suhani.
I'm a BFA student specialising in Applied Art at Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun. I enjoy experimenting with design, drawing, painting, and visual ideas. As a student, I'm continuously learning new techniques and developing my creative style.

My goal is to grow as a creative designer, make meaningful visual work, and keep learning through projects, class activities, and new experiences. Whether through traditional mediums or digital tools, I love transforming abstract thoughts into tangible art.
