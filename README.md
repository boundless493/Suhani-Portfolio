# Suhani Dhirwan — BFA Applied Art Portfolio

An editorial minimalist dark portfolio website created for **Suhani Dhirwan**, BFA student specialising in Applied Art at **Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun**, combining the reference layout aesthetic with authentic university coursework, artwork, and verified certificates.

---

## 🌟 Key Updates & Features

1. **Real Project Images Added Respectively**:
   - **Himalayan Mountain Village — Canvas Painting**: High-resolution photography of Suhani's traditional Pahadi stone-and-timber village landscape painting.
   - **Himalayan Monal — BFA Studio Artwork**: Authentic canvas painting of the Himalayan Monal with original artist signature plate (*"Suhani, BFA 1st Semester, Batch: 2024-28, Department of Fine Art, GEHU"*).
   - **MakeMyTrip Corporate Stationery & Business Card**: Applied Art commercial stationery design featuring custom visiting card and corporate envelope layouts.
   - **MakeMyTrip Official Letterhead & Paris Skyline Art**: Detailed architectural vector skyline illustrations (Eiffel Tower, Arc de Triomphe, Notre Dame) on official corporate letterhead.
   - **Observational Illustration & Anatomy Drawing**: Fine linework and gesture studies.
   - **Visual Storytelling & Narrative Concept Art**: Thumbnail storyboards and character expressions.

2. **Direct Interactive Certificate Viewer (Zero Google Drive Redirects)**:
   - When clicking on **"✦ View Certificates"** or on any certificate card, the site opens an in-page **Lightbox Modal Viewer**.
   - Includes **5 Verified Certificates & Awards**:
     1. **Grafest 2025: Petri Plate Art** — Certificate of Participation, Graphic Era University, Dehradun
     2. **Pariksha Pe Charcha 2023** — Official letter of appreciation from Prime Minister Narendra Modi
     3. **Gender Sensitization & Legal Awareness Competition** — Certificate of Merit, National Commission for Women & PM Shri Kendriya Vidyalaya IIP, Dehradun
     4. **Background Design: Landscapes & Environments** — Certificate of Completion, Udemy (Instructor: Siobhan Twomey)
     5. **Strategies for Sustainable Design (Elite Score: 65%)** — NPTEL / SWAYAM / IIT Hyderabad / Skill India / Ministry of Education, Govt. of India
   - Features quick-switch navigation buttons (`← Previous` / `Next →`), clickable tabs, and a direct link to inspect the full 4000px original image.

3. **Performance & Interaction**:
   - Fast native hardware-accelerated scrolling with zero lag.
   - Standard system mouse pointer.
   - Working contact form with validation and 1-click email copy.
